Is Roswell, New Mexico about to spend $1,196,308 on soccer field lighting without a public bid? The paperwork says the city plans to buy through a cooperative contract. That is a different path than a bid.

The City of Roswell's [Infrastructure Committee agenda for Monday, Nov. 16, 2026](https://www.roswell-nm.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_11162026-3135) lists item 4. The committee was asked to consider recommending a budget adjustment of $1,196,308.00 for sports lighting at the Cielo Grande Soccer Complex. Staff's recommendation is to proceed as presented. The packet calls this an initial consideration, so it is not a final council vote.

The city's stated reason: the complex can't be used in the cooler parts of the day, or for long stretches of the year, because it has no lights. The money would move from the recreation fund's pooled cash to a land improvements account.

The purchase would run through Musco's Sourcewell contract, number 041123-MSL. The attached Musco quote is dated Sept. 22, 2026, and lists the turnkey price with installation by Snider Electric. It says New Mexico gross receipts tax is not included. The quote shows an expiration date of Oct. 22, 2026, and says pricing holds 30 days. It also lists 8 to 10 weeks for delivery after order and submittal approval. The packet doesn't say whether the quote has been refreshed.

The quote puts some work on the buyer: electrical distribution design and materials, and getting power to the site.

This brief covers only what the agenda packet shows. See more in our [government contracts coverage](/news/category/gov-contracts), or our earlier brief on [a GSA energy contract](/news/gsa-energy-contract-led-retrofits).

**Why it matters:** Cooperative contracts like Sourcewell let agencies skip a bid, and the installer can be named in the quote. If you want public sports or site lighting work, get approved with the contract holder early. Also read what the quote leaves to the owner, like site power and distribution, and price that part.
