Is the Johnson Level digital angle locator in your truck part of a recall? It might be. On Nov. 19, 2026, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced that Hultafors Group North America is recalling about 229,430 trade tools because kids can get at the button batteries inside.

The [CPSC notice (recall number 27-090)](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Hultafors-Group-North-America-Recalls-Lighted-Portfolios-Angle-Locators-Calipers-and-Calculators-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Battery-Ingestion-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Consumer-Products-with-Button-Cell-Batteries) says the tools break the federal safety standard for products with button cell and coin batteries. The packaging and products also lack the warnings required under Reese's Law.

What's covered, per CPSC:

- Custom Leather Craft Lighted Portfolio, model L217
- Johnson Level digital angle locators, models 1750-1200, 1886-0100 and 1886-0200
- Johnson Level 7" and 11" angle locator and ruler, models 1888-0700 and 1888-1100
- Johnson Level Digital Caliper, model 1889-0600
- Johnson Level 21" Digital Magnetic Level and Angle Locator with Dot Laser, model 40-6065
- Johnson Level Building Calculator, model CALC-1500

CPSC says they were sold at Lowe's, Menards, W.W. Grainger, True Value and other hardware stores, and on Amazon, from February 2023 through November 2026. Prices ran from about $45 to $400.

The fix is a refund, not a repair. The company's recall FAQ says there are no replacement parts. You stop using the tool, keep it away from children, and upload a photo at the company's recall site, HG2026.expertinquiry.com. Once approved, you get the retail price as a virtual Visa gift card. CPSC lists the company's phone line as 877-722-7026.

The CPSC page lists no injury count. The recall is about the battery access and missing warnings.

More recalls are in our [recalls and safety coverage](/news/category/recalls-safety). Earlier we covered [the Lithonia RSX tilt arm recall](/news/lithonia-rsx-tilt-arm-recall).

**Why it matters:** These are layout and measuring tools. They ride on job trucks and end up on school and facility job sites. Check model numbers against the CPSC list. Keep a photo and the recall number in your safety file if a facility manager or GC asks. The company says there are no replacement parts, so budget for new tools.
