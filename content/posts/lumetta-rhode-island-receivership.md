Providence Business News reported on November 13 that Lumetta Inc., a Warwick, Rhode Island maker of custom commercial luminaires, has been placed under state receivership. The company filed last month. PBN said the proceedings began October 16.

Receivership, as PBN described it, is the step used when a business is treated as insolvent and a court puts a neutral third party in charge. PBN’s own research put the headcount at about 50. The public write-up calls Lumetta an architectural lighting manufacturer that makes custom commercial luminaires.

That is the usable fact set. The article is behind a subscription wall past the opening, so this brief does not add a sale date, a buyer, or a reason the filing happened. Those details are not in the portion PBN published for free, and we are not filling them in.

What a contractor can do with the public facts is narrower and more useful. Lumetta’s work is custom commercial product, not a commodity high bay sitting on a distributor shelf. If a spec names that maker, the open question is whether the company can still build, ship, and stand behind the fixture while a receiver is in place. That is a question for the specifier and for whoever holds the warranty, not a guess from a news brief.

Inside Lighting flagged the same filing on X on November 14 and pointed readers at the PBN story. We are using the PBN report as the source, and only the facts that report put in the open.
