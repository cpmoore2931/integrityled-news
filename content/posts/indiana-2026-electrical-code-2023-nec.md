Which NEC does Indiana use now? The 2023 edition, even though the new rule is called the Indiana Electrical Code, 2026 Edition.

The Fire Prevention and Building Safety Commission filed the rule (LSA Document #26-62) on October 23, 2026. The Indiana Register posted it on November 18. The [published rule](https://iar.iga.in.gov/register/20261118-IR-675260062FRA) adopts the 2023 NEC by reference and repeals the old rule, 675 IAC 17-1.8. The notice says it takes effect 30 days after filing. By my count that is about November 22, but confirm the date with your inspector.

Here are some of the state changes in the text:

- **AFCI.** Sections 210.12(B) and 210.12(E) are deleted. The commission points to a new state law that bars rules requiring AFCIs in certain housing built after June 30, 2026.
- **GFCI, outdoor outlets.** The expiration date on the HVAC exception in 210.8(F) is removed. The commission cited concerns about availability of the new technology.
- **Surge protection.** The dwelling-unit item in 215.18(A) is deleted, and the commission also removed the surge requirements in 215.18(D) and 230.67.
- **Emergency disconnect.** Section 230.85 is deleted. A commenter asked to keep it for first responders, and the commission said no.
- **Garages.** The 210.52(G)(1) exception now covers garage spaces not attached to a multifamily unit through a dedicated door.

The commission also turned down a request to bring in 2026 NEC language on inverter-based systems in 702.4(A), saying it would not fit the 2023 base.

I only read the rule text and the commission's responses. I did not check how local permits already in the pipeline are handled.

If you work in several states, see [The NEC edition on a lighting job is a local question](/news/nec-edition-is-a-local-question). More in [code updates](/news/category/code-updates).

**Why it matters:** On Indiana jobs, draw and price to the 2023 NEC plus the Indiana amendments, and confirm the effective date and any permit-in-progress rules with your local inspector.
