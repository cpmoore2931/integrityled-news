Are your RSX area lights part of a recall? If they hang on an adjustable tilt arm, they might be. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission ([CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Lithonia-Lighting-Recalls-RSX-Luminaires-with-Adjustable-Tilt-Arm-Mountings-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Impact-Hazard)) posted the recall on October 22, 2026. It covers about 11,532 Lithonia Lighting RSX luminaires.

**The problem.** CPSC says the knuckle on the tilt arm can fail and let the fixture fall. That is an impact hazard.

**What to look for.** Four mount options are recalled. Look for these codes in the catalog number:

- AASP: square pole mount
- AARP: round pole mount
- AAWB: wall bracket
- AAWSC: wall bracket with conduit box

**The numbers, per CPSC.** Lithonia received 13 reports of arms breaking. In four of them, the fixture fell. No injuries were reported. The units were sold through commercial distributors, including Turtle & Hughes and Consolidated Electrical Distributors, from August 2019 to August 2026. Prices ran $400 to $2,200.

**Dates differ.** CPSC lists mounts made August 2019 to July 2020 and August 2024 to April 2026. Lithonia's own FAQ lists August 19, 2019 to July 31, 2020, and August 1, 2024 to June 16, 2026, and says mounts used with RSX1 and RSXF1 are not included. So do not guess. Have Lithonia confirm each site.

**The fix.** It is free. Lithonia sends a qualified professional to swap the arm for a new complete adjustable arm assembly. The fixture itself is not recalled. Until then, CPSC says keep people clear of the area directly under the light. Claims go through Lithonia at 855-307-2454, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET, Monday to Friday, or the Recalls link on lithonia.acuitybrands.com.

**Why it matters:** If you hung RSX lights on tilt arms, check the job records for those codes and call your customers. See more in [recalls and safety](/news/category/recalls-safety).
