Who won the Pearl City High School auditorium lighting job? D.Y.C. Electric Srvc LLC. The Hawaii State Department of Education issued a Notice of Award on November 9, 2026, for $2 million, according to a press release from the Hawaii State Senate Majority.

Senator Brandon Elefante announced the award. The release says construction is expected to start after a pre-construction conference. That meeting is meant to settle scheduling, site access and other project requirements. No start date was given in the release.

The scope is the useful part. The project is called the Auditorium Lighting Renovation Project at the Michael D. Nakasone Performing Arts Center. The release lists replacement of existing lighting systems throughout the auditorium: house lighting, general work lighting, lighting controls and distribution, production lighting fixtures, and emergency lighting.

That is a whole-system job, not a lamp swap. Controls, distribution and emergency lighting sit in the same package as the fixtures.

The bid side lines up. A public bid listing summarized by Starbridge shows the Hawaii DOE posted the solicitation (DOE Job No. Q84127-23) on May 20, 2026, with bids closing June 19, 2026. That listing describes the same replacement scope. A separate award notice on BidDetail also shows the $2 million value and the November 9 date.

The release does not name fixture brands, the number of fixtures, or the bid tab. It also does not say how many bidders there were. This brief does not guess at any of that.
